What awaits you

Quick rounds, lots to discover – and every level plays a little differently.

Every level is a countdown

Blast as many asteroids as you can before time runs out. Every hit earns bonus points at the end – the clock starts with the first rock.

Fire stages & colorful stars

The yellow star powers up your weapons – all the way to a double burst. Green shatters asteroids into fragments. And red? Better dodge it.

Bonus round in the tunnel

From level 4, destroy more than 100 asteroids and a 3D tunnel opens up – with free flight controls and its own rules.

Offline & ad-free

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your high scores stay on your device. Just launch and play.

The enemies

Which enemies show up changes from level to level. Each has its own tricks – and some have something to give away.

SPACE INVADER

Send it spinning and shoot down its ring of cubes – and it hands you its shield. When two show up, the second one sets off a shockwave.

SPACE PROBE

Carries three colorful stars. Hit it twice and it lets them go – catch the right ones.

BALLWALL

A wall of balls rolls toward you. Shoot the door open and fly through.

TECHNOSPHERE

Hides behind a ring of shield balls – and fires your own shots right back at you.

BIGFLASH

A lightning vortex that pulls in your shots. Careful: misses can curve back around!

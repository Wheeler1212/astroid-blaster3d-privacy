Last updated: September 16, 2026 · applies to the iOS app “Astroid Blaster3D”

Short version: Astroid Blaster3D does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no user account, no tracking, no advertising, and no analytics. Anything the app remembers as a setting stays on your device only.

02 What data is processed?

None. Astroid Blaster3D is a fully offline game and works without an internet connection. It does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other kind of personal data — to me or to anyone else.

The app only remembers a handful of technical gameplay settings (for example, whether a debug overlay is switched on) using Apple's local storage mechanism, UserDefaults. This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is never shared with me, with Apple, or with any third party,
  • is deleted when you uninstall the app.

03 No Third-Party Services

The app does not include any third-party software for data collection. Specifically, it does not use:

  • Analytics or tracking services (e.g. Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising networks
  • Game Center, cloud saves, or online leaderboards
  • Social media integrations or sign-in providers
  • In-app purchases or payment services

04 No User Account

Astroid Blaster3D requires no registration, no account, and no sign-in. You can start playing immediately after installing it, without providing any information.

05 Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any data at all, this applies regardless of the player's age — no personal data is processed for children either.

06 Your Rights

Since no personal data is processed, requests for access, correction, or deletion under Art. 15–17 GDPR have nothing to act on — there simply isn't anything stored. If you have any questions about privacy in this app regardless, you're welcome to reach me at the email address above at any time.

07 Changes to this Policy

Should the app's data handling ever change (for example, through a future update adding online leaderboards), this page will be updated accordingly and the date above revised.

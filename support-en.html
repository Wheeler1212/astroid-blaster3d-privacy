02 FAQ

How do I control the ship?

Swipe on the right half of the screen to move the ship. Tap on the left half to fire – use two fingers to do both at once.

How long does a level last?

Each level has a fixed time that starts with the first asteroid. At the end you get bonus points for every asteroid you destroyed. Some enemies even give you extra seconds.

How do I reach the bonus round?

From level 4 on: destroy more than 100 asteroids in a single level.

What do the colorful stars do?

The yellow star boosts your firepower, the green one makes asteroids shatter into many fragments – and the red one costs a life. Unless you're wearing a shield …

Where are my high scores stored?

Only on your device, separately for each difficulty level. Deleting the app also deletes your high scores. You can change your player name in the high score section.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Astroid Blaster3D is a fully offline game.

How do I turn off sound or music?

Via “Settings” in the main menu.
